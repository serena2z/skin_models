QuickSegment

  • Click the "Choose files" button and choose a CSV file to load your images.
  • Click to add markings to the image. (Hold Shift to keep the square aligned with the axes)
  • Move through the list of images with the << and >> buttons, or with the Q and E keys.
  • Use Z/X to zoom in and out, and WASD to move around the image
  • Press R to toggle whether the image is included in the exported dataset.
  • You can use the Reset button to bring back the original bounding box, if one exists
